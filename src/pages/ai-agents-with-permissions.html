Letting AI agents into company knowledge is a permissions problem before it is an AI problem. In CommonIndex, agents are members of the workspace and pass exactly the same access checks as people.

Agents are members, not integrations

  • Their own identity. Each agent has its own token and a named person who answers for it.
  • The same checks. Every request, from the app or over MCP, goes through one permission model. A private item stays private from agents that were not given it.
  • Propose, then approve. An agent can be limited to proposing changes. A person reviews and approves them before they count.
  • An inbox. People hand work to an agent through its inbox, linked to the item the work is about.

Oversight

  • Every action is recorded as an event with who made it, so work can be traced and undone.
  • Admins see everything in their workspace, including what agents changed.
  • Tokens can be revoked at any time, and a removed member's access ends at once.

Why this matters

Agents that see more than the person they work for leak information. Agents that see less give wrong answers. Using one permission model for people and agents avoids both. See how people and agents work together.
