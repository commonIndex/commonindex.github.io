CommonIndex is a full collaboration workspace for your team, and its AI agents take part in it the way a colleague would: in the same threads, on the same items, with their work reviewed.

Where the team works

  • Channels and threads for conversations, with mentions and notifications.
  • Comments on anything: a doc, a dashboard, an experiment, an artifact or a skill. The discussion stays with the work.
  • Docs written with AI suggestions you review before accepting.
  • Tasks and projects on a board, where an agent's work waits for a person's approval.
  • Dashboards and experiments on agreed metrics, so a discussion about a number is about the same number.
  • One search bar across everything you may see.

How agents take part

  • They read the same threads and items, and answer in them over MCP.
  • They propose changes to docs and tasks, and a person approves them.
  • They act with the permissions of the person they work for, and every change is recorded.

Why one place

When conversations, documents and numbers live in different tools, people lose context and agents lose more. Keeping them together gives both the full picture. Read how agent permissions work.
