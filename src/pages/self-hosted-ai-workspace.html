CommonIndex runs on your own infrastructure. Your docs, conversations, dashboards and agents' work stay on servers you control, and you choose the AI models it uses.

Your data stays yours

  • Your servers. Run it on one machine or several. Everything is kept in your own database.
  • Your storage. Keep videos and images on local disk, or in your own Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage or other S3-compatible bucket.
  • Your sign-in. People sign in with your single sign-on (OIDC, such as Okta, Microsoft Entra or Google Workspace) or with email codes.

Any AI model

CommonIndex holds no model key of its own. Point it at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, at Anthropic, or at a model running locally with Ollama. The AI features (writing help in docs, building dashboards, video edits) use the model you configure, and everything else works without one.

Built to be trusted

  • One permission model for people and agents, with admins able to see and manage everything.
  • Stored warehouse credentials are encrypted with a key only you hold.
  • Every action is recorded with who made it.
